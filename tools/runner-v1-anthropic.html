Interview runner

Administers the fixed schedule against fresh model instances, three conditions by three wordings, and writes out every exchange verbatim. Each run starts from an empty context: no instance sees another's answers, and none of them sees this page.

What this does not do. It collects. It does not analyse. Reading the transcripts, segmenting each description into the stages the model names, and comparing those stages across instances is the part that has to be yours, and it is the part the article stands on.
