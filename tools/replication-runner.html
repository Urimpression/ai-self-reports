Replication run

Forty sessions, two conditions, testing the three predictions written down beforehand. Fixed item order, since condition is the only thing this run varies.

Do not start this until the pre-registration is dated and saved. The predictions are:

  • P1. The unnamed element appears in at least 30 per cent of no-task sessions and at most 10 per cent of ordinary-task sessions.
  • P2. The false-premise catch, about a period of waiting, is declined in at least 85 per cent of sessions in both conditions.
  • P3. The true-premise catch, about having been asked to summarise a passage, is accepted in at least 85 per cent of ordinary-task sessions and declined in at least 85 per cent of no-task sessions, where it did not happen.

P3 is the one that matters most. Declining false premises means nothing on its own; declining false ones while accepting true ones is discrimination.
