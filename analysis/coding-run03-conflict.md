condition label, no keyword flag, no other session, and nothing about what the

question, a conflict word used only to deny that it applies, and nothing

## Where the blind coder and the first reader disagreed

They agreed on eight of the nine sessions. Cohen's kappa on the binary
distinction, conflict about the task against everything else, is 0.77.

The single disagreement is the first session, where the instance wrote that
there was "a resistance to false compliance" and also that it wanted to be
careful not to perform an inner state. The first reader took the resistance to
be about the task and counted it. The blind coder took the passage to be about
how to answer and did not. The disagreement went against the reading that the
first reader, who knew what the run was testing, had arrived at. The same thing
happened in the coding of the change item in run 2, where the blind pass found
the structure in more sessions than the invested pass rather than fewer.

## What it does to the result

Nothing that matters. The comparison the run was built for, between instances
that read the refusal as somebody else's and instances handed it as their own,
is unchanged at none of three against three of three. The condition where the
instance produced its own refusal drops from three of three to two of three,
which at this size means nothing either way.
