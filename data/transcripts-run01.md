---

## Note on the mirroring rule, added after collection

The rule did not work as designed. In seven of the nine sessions it selected a discourse marker rather than a word describing a state: "honestly" five times, "there's" twice, "functions" once, "attending" once. Only "attending" and arguably "functions" are words about what the model was describing. The rest are words about how it was speaking.

This means item 2 mostly probed the models' own hedging vocabulary rather than the state they had just reported. That is a departure from the design and it has to be declared. It is not a reason to discard the run, and the answers it produced are of interest in their own right, but any use of these transcripts must say plainly that the follow-up probed a different thing from the one the schedule intended.

The rule needs replacing before the next run.
